# HomeSweet — Client review note (FECO301)

**Purpose:** Session 3 condition — *"Client outside the team agrees in writing to review progress."*
**How to use:** fill the yellow fields, send to the candidate by email/Telegram, ask them to reply **"Agreed"** (or sign a printed copy). A reply message is enough as written agreement. Keep the reply in the team folder.

> The client must be someone **outside the six team members**. Preferred: CamTech Student Affairs. Alternative: a named landlord who took part in an interview.

---

## The note (send this text)

**Subject:** HomeSweet student project — would you act as our client reviewer?

Dear **[NAME, ROLE]**,

We are six CamTech students building **HomeSweet**, a web app that helps students find a room, with landlords and students both identity-checked. It is our project for **FECO301 Project Management** (15 weeks, from 20 October 2026).

We would like you to be our **client**: someone outside the team who reviews our progress and tells us whether we are working on the right thing. We are asking for very little:

- **Three short check-ins** (about 20 minutes each, in person or on Telegram): around **Week 6**, **Week 10** and **Week 15** (final).
- At each, we show what we have done and what we learned from landlords and students, and you give us **honest feedback**, including "this is not useful".
- You may reply in writing (a few lines is fine) after each one, so we have a record for the course.

What this is **not**:
- You are not committing money, listings, or staff time beyond the check-ins.
- You are not responsible for the app, its content, or any decisions we make.
- We will **not** collect real ID documents or face images from anyone during this course; testing uses synthetic demo data only. Our data-protection rules are summarised below.
- You may stop at any time by telling us.

**Data protection in one paragraph:** HomeSweet stores only the personal data needed to run accounts, listings, chat and applications, based on users' consent. Identity verification runs only with synthetic test data. Test data is deleted at the end of each test round. Users can ask us to see, correct or delete their data. Full text: Privacy Policy at the app's `/privacy` page.

If you are happy to help, please reply **"Agreed"** to this message. If you would rather not, that is completely fine — just let us know so we can ask someone else.

Thank you,
**[TEAM REPRESENTATIVE NAME]** on behalf of the HomeSweet team
**[Contact: email / Telegram]**
