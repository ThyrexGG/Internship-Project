# Landlord interview guide (need ≥3)
**Purpose:** Thomas: landlords are half the marketplace and there is **no evidence from them yet**. Ask listing, verification, and paying. Their answers decide if the idea works.

**Rule:** notes only. Do not collect ID photos. Get verbal consent to use anonymised quotes in the charter/A1.

**Time:** 20–30 minutes. In person or Telegram. Record: date, district, type of stock (rooms / whole unit), how they fill vacancies today.

---

## Opening
We are CamTech students. HomeSweet is a course project: a web app where students find rooms. We are **not** selling you a listing today. We need to know if landlords would use a verified listing process.

## Questions (ask all three blocks)

### A. Listing
1. How do you find tenants now (Facebook, agent, friends, walk-in)?
2. What is painful about that?
3. Would you put a room on a student website if CamTech students used it? Why / why not?
4. What would you need to see before you bother (how many students, who runs it, contract)?

### B. Verification
5. Would you accept **your** listing being checked (photo of the room, visit, or documents)?
6. Would you require **students** to be identity-checked before viewing or depositing?
7. What is too much (ID card copies stored online, face scan, university email only)?

### C. Paying
8. Who should pay for this: you, the student, or the university?
9. Would you pay a listing fee / success fee / nothing?
10. Ballpark: $0 / $5–20/month / one month’s rent commission / other?

### Close
11. Would you review our progress this semester in writing (client role)? Yes / no / maybe.  
12. May we contact you again in Weeks 6 and 10?
