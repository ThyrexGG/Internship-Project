## Part A — Manifesto (what we stand for)

*Proposed; the team rewrites in its own words.*

1. **We build trust, not just features.** If a choice makes the product more impressive but less honest, we choose honest.
2. **We never touch real people's identity data for a grade.** Test data only; see Part E.
3. **We say what we don't know.** Open questions go in the plan, not under the carpet.
4. **We share the hard and the boring work.** Nobody is permanently the person who writes up, or the person who codes.
5. **We tell each other early.** A problem raised on Monday is a schedule change; on Friday it is a crisis.
6. **We disagree in the open and decide once.** After a decision is logged, everyone commits to it.

## Part C — How we work

**Meetings.** Weekly team meeting: [day, time, place/online]. 30–45 minutes. Agenda: last week's tasks, blockers, next week's tasks, decisions. Minutes by [rotating — start with name], posted in [channel] within 24 hours.

**Communication.** Main channel: [Telegram group / other]. Reply to direct questions within [24] hours on weekdays. Urgent: [call]. Quiet hours: [e.g. 22:00–07:00].

**Decisions.** Day-to-day: the task owner decides. Anything that changes scope, schedule, budget, ethics or the client: team decision by [majority of 6 / consensus]; recorded in the decision log (Gantt *Assumptions* or the shared log) **within 24 hours**. A tie is decided by [project coordinator / coin / client's view].

**Definition of done.** A task is done when: the deliverable is saved in the team folder with its agreed name; a second member has read or tested it; the Gantt hours are updated honestly.

**Time tracking.** Each member logs hours weekly in [sheet]. We review the totals at each client check-in (Weeks 6, 10, 15). Targets are ≈5 h/week; a bad week is allowed, a bad month needs a conversation.

## Part D — When things go wrong

**Missed work.** Tell the group **before** the deadline. Another member may cover; hours are rebalanced in the Gantt *Team* sheet. Repeated misses are handled by the steps below, not by silently redoing the work.

**Steps for persistent problems (one member not delivering, conflict, etc.):**
1. Direct, private conversation between the people involved (within one week).
2. Raise at the team meeting with facts (tasks, dates), not character.
3. Written agreement of what changes and by when.
4. If it continues two more weeks, involve the lecturer (Thomas) with the log of steps 1–3.

**Leaving the team or illness.** Tell the coordinator and lecturer promptly. Remaining members re-plan using the capacity check on the Gantt; scope is cut before hours are inflated.

**Credit.** Individual contribution is documented in the log and the learning-goal sheets; the team submits one honest account of who did what.

## Part E — Data, ethics and code commitments

By signing, each member agrees:

- **No real ID documents, face images or national ID numbers** are collected, stored or demonstrated at any time. Test sessions use the synthetic demo ID only. (See `docs/legal/01_DATA_PROTECTION_POLICY.md`.)
- Usability participants are recorded only as `HS-P01…`; no names, no student numbers; no recordings without separate written consent.
- Admin access, API keys and `.env` values are **not** shared in public channels or committed to git. Admin accounts are limited to named members.
- Nobody switches `VUE_APP_KYC_MODE` to `live`, or deploys a rules change, without the team's logged decision.
- We do not misrepresent the product: no "legally binding", "escrow", "guaranteed", "first in Cambodia" claims (see `docs/legal/02_LEASE_AGREEMENT_REVIEW.md`).
- Code goes through the repo conventions in `docs/PROJECT_STATE.md` §11 (lint clean, commit message format, no two people editing the same file at once).
- We attribute sources and AI assistance honestly in submitted work, as the course requires.
